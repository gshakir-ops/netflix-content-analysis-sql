📅 August 2026

🎯 Sales Analytics

This project demonstrates comprehensive business intelligence capabilities through the development of an enterprise-grade e-commerce analytics dashboard. The dashboard was built using Power BI to consolidate and analyze sales performance data across multiple dimensions including customer behavior, product categories, regional performance, and temporal trends spanning multiple sales cycles.

The dashboard architecture incorporates advanced data modeling techniques including star schema design, dimensional tables for products, customers, regions, and time periods, and fact tables capturing transactional metrics. Dynamic filtering and drill-through capabilities enable stakeholders to explore data at multiple granularity levels—from high-level KPIs down to individual transaction details—supporting both strategic decision-making and operational analysis.

Key analytical capabilities include real-time KPI tracking (revenue, order count, average order value), time-series trend analysis revealing seasonal patterns and growth trajectories, geographic performance mapping identifying regional opportunities, product category performance comparison, and customer segmentation analysis. The dashboard implements sophisticated DAX measures for year-over-year comparisons, running totals, period-over-period growth calculations, and complex business logic. This project showcases the ability to transform raw e-commerce data into intuitive, interactive visualizations that drive business growth and operational excellence.

Project Objectives

  • Centralized Analytics: Create a single source of truth for e-commerce sales data accessible to all stakeholders.
  • Performance Tracking: Enable real-time monitoring of key sales metrics and KPIs for quick decision-making.
  • Trend Identification: Uncover seasonal patterns, growth opportunities, and underperforming areas.
  • Customer Intelligence: Provide insights into customer behavior, preferences, and lifetime value.
  • Regional Strategy: Support regional sales teams with location-specific performance data.

Technical Implementation

  • Data Modeling: Designed star schema with fact and dimension tables for optimal query performance.
  • DAX Measures: Created advanced calculated measures for YoY growth, running totals, and period comparisons.
  • Dynamic Filtering: Implemented slicers and cross-filtering for interactive data exploration.
  • Custom Visuals: Utilized Power BI custom visuals for enhanced data storytelling and insights.
  • Mobile Optimization: Designed mobile-responsive layout for dashboard access on any device.

Key Analytical Insights

  • Peak Performance Periods: Identified Q4 as the strongest quarter with consistent year-over-year growth patterns.
  • Top Categories: Electronics and Fashion categories driving majority of revenue with high customer engagement.
  • Geographic Patterns: Regional analysis revealed untapped markets and optimization opportunities for logistics.
  • Customer Behavior: Discovered customer purchasing cycles enabling targeted marketing campaigns.

Business Impact

  • Data-Driven Culture: Empowered stakeholders with self-service analytics reducing dependency on manual reports.
  • Faster Decisions: Real-time dashboard access enabled quicker response to market changes and opportunities.
  • Resource Optimization: Insights led to better inventory management and targeted marketing spend.
  • Scalable Solution: Dashboard architecture designed to accommodate growing data volumes and new requirements.
